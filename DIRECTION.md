## Brief
- **Who buys:** Swansea homeowners (plus some commercial clients) wanting a driveway, patio, garden,
  plastering, rendering or a repair. They decide on photos of real jobs and on what neighbours say.
- **Proof to surface:** the 18 job photos (three are before/after pairs), the three named reviews,
  "30+ years experience", the 14-trade list.
- **Conversion path:** tap-to-call in the header on every page, "Get a free quote" button everywhere,
  a contact page with service tick-boxes (`?service=<slug>` pre-ticks).
- **Structure:** two arms, one firm — **Building** and the new **Landscaping** (Bowles & Wyer split their
  offer into clear arms; Stonemarket names products the way homeowners search: "Driveways", "Garden paving").
- **Look:** the photo is the hero (Landform, Tom Stuart-Smith), with one confident brand-colour flood
  (Bowles & Wyer's full-bleed green) — here the logo's navy.
- **Signature idea:** the work being laid — herringbone block paving laying itself across a real job.

## Three directions
| | A. Laid by hand (material) | B. House-mark film (cinematic) | C. Two arms catalogue (precise) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Palette | Logo navy, sandstone, limestone white | Deep navy + white only | White, navy text, grey cards |
| Type | Bricolage Grotesque 800 + Geist | Bricolage 800 + Geist | Geist only |
| Wow | Herringbone pavers lay themselves over a before photo to reveal the after | 3D plot (house, drive, patio, fence, decking) assembles with a service list | Split-screen Build / Landscape slider |
| Flow | Hero photo → two arms → wow → trades → projects → reviews → sandstone quote band | Logo-window intro → 3D plot → projects → reviews | Category grid → projects → form |

Scores (art director / owner / phone buyer + perf):
- **A: 8 / 9 / 9 = 26** — unmistakably a paving-and-building firm; cheap to run (2D canvas).
- **B: 9 / 8 / 7 = 24** — most "wow", heavier (Three.js), less tied to their real photos.
- **C: 6 / 7 / 9 = 22** — fast and clear, but a tidy template; that has been rejected before.

**Winner: A.** Borrow B's house-mark-as-window intro for the hero and C's two-arm split for navigation.
Both wow moments are built as candidates (`/wow-a` laid paving, `/wow-b` 3D plot) for the owner to pick.
